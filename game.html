Sky Shards

Guide

How to play Sky Shards

Everyone online gets the same floating islands today. Your run starts on the big central island. The clock runs until you pick up the last crystal shard. Fastest time wins the day.

Goal

  • Find and mine every crystal shard. The counter top-left shows how many are left.
  • Most islands have one shard on top. Big islands also have one hanging underneath, so look below.
  • Each shard gives +12 clouds.

Radar

  • The radar top-right is a map of the islands that turns with you. Up on the radar is where you’re looking.
  • Cyan dots are shards. ▲ means it’s above you, ▼ below, a dot means about your level.
  • The pulsing ring marks the nearest shard. Shards beyond range sit on the edge of the radar.

Controls

  • WASD move, Space jump, double-tap W to sprint.
  • Shift sneak. While sneaking you can’t fall off an edge.
  • Hold left mouse to mine. Stone and logs take longer than dirt, leaves and clouds.
  • Right mouse places the selected block. Hold it to place several.
  • 1–7 or the mouse wheel pick a block. M mutes sound. Esc pauses.

Clouds and bridges

  • You start with 20 clouds. They are the fastest way across the void.
  • A cloud melts 20 seconds after you place it. At 15 seconds it turns see-through as a warning.
  • Mining any block puts it in your hotbar, so dirt, stone and logs work as bridges that never melt.
  • Falling into the void returns you to the last island you stood on and costs 4 clouds.

How to bridge fast

  1. Walk to the edge of the island and hold Shift.
  2. Turn around so the void is behind you and look down at the side of the block you stand on.
  3. Walk backwards with S and click right mouse to place a block each step. Sneaking keeps you from falling.
  4. To go up, jump and place a block under your feet while in the air.

Leaderboard

  • Your nickname and time go to today and all time boards when you finish.
  • Only your best run counts. The world changes every day at 00:00 UTC (03:00 Kyiv).
  • Runs under 20 seconds or faster than real time are rejected.
